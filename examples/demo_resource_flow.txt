Example only: CPU -> Video -> Storage -> Video
1. CPU: VFS open /games/demo/sprites.bin and map extents.
2. CPU: Video ALLOC_RESOURCE(size, type) -> handle
3. CPU: Storage PREPARE_EXTENTS(transfer_id, list)
4. CPU: Video PREPARE_RECEIVE(transfer_id, handle)
5. CPU: select OWNER(Video), TARGET(Storage), hardware GRANT.
6. Video SPI master clocks READ(transfer_id) from Storage SPI slave.
7. Video releases external drivers; ATTN_N.
8. CPU reclaims bus, polls Video+Storage, confirms CRC and READY.
9. CPU: Video DRAW_SPRITE(handle, x, y)
